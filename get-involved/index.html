Before you give

A few quick answers.

Need help with a transfer? Contact the team

What will my donation support?

Meals, school essentials, clothing, hygiene supplies, and community care. Ask the team about current priorities if you want to support a particular need.

Which number should I use for a transfer?

Use +256 703 259 561 for WorldRemit or Sendwave. Our separate enquiry number is +256 705 588 799. Confirm the recipient details with the team before sending.

How can I confirm my donation?

Keep your provider's payment confirmation and message the team with the date, amount, and reference if you need help matching your transfer. Never share your password or full card details.

Can I donate supplies instead?

Yes. Contact the team first to confirm useful items and delivery arrangements. Our donation guide explains how to prepare.

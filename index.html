Who we are

Built by community, for community

Practical support, organized close to home.

Gimbo Children's Foundation works with families and local leaders to respond to needs that affect a child's safety, health, learning, and dignity.

Each visit begins by listening. The team then coordinates useful support and stays connected to the people involved.

Our work

Support around the whole child

Food, school supplies, and family support.

Families rarely face one challenge at a time. These programs are planned together around needs confirmed in the community.

01

Food & nutrition

Regular, balanced meals help ensure hunger does not stand in the way of a school day or a normal childhood.

↗
02

Education support

Books, uniforms, supplies, meals, transport support, and mentorship can reduce practical barriers to attending school.

↗
03

Clothing & hygiene

Clothing, shoes, and hygiene essentials support children's health, comfort, and dignity.

↗
04

Mentorship & spiritual encouragement

Time with trusted adults gives children space for conversation, play, and encouragement.

↗
05

Family outreach

Visits and follow-up connect support to each family's circumstances and priorities.

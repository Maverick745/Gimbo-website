Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy covers the Gimbo Children's Foundation website and information you choose to share with our team through the contact and donation options on it. The foundation is based in Gimbo village, Buikwe, Uganda. For a privacy question or request, contact the team on WhatsApp at +256 705 588 799.

Information collected when you visit

The website does not contain an enquiry form, newsletter signup, advertising pixels, or visitor analytics added by the foundation. Our hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser, requested pages, and security logs to deliver and protect the website. A restricted preview may also require the hosting provider's sign-in service.

Messages and donations

If you contact us on WhatsApp, we receive your phone number, profile information visible in WhatsApp, and the message or attachments you send. We use that information to answer you and coordinate the support, visit, or activity you ask about. Please send only the information needed for your enquiry.

Donations are handled by PayPal, WorldRemit, or Sendwave outside this website. We do not collect card details or payment passwords here. The provider or donor may share a name, amount, transaction reference, and confirmation with the team so a donation can be identified and recorded. Do not send us a PIN, password, or one-time security code.

Why information is used and shared

Information is used to respond to requests, coordinate activities and donations, keep relevant records, and meet applicable legal obligations. Only share information about another person when you have permission or another lawful reason. Relevant information may be handled by team members coordinating your request and the communication, payment, or hosting service involved. These services may process information outside Uganda under their own policies.

Cookies and external services

The foundation's site code does not set advertising or analytics cookies or store visitor profiles in your browser. Hosting security or sign-in services may use necessary cookies. When you follow a WhatsApp or payment link, the destination service's privacy and cookie rules apply. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

Documentary playback

The homepage documentary connects to YouTube only when you press Play. Playback uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain. YouTube may then receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details and process information under its own privacy and cookie policies. You may instead follow the Watch on YouTube link to view it on that service.

Photographs and children's information

Photographs and videos document community activities. They must not be reused to identify, contact, profile, or exploit a child. If you or a child in your care appears in material that concerns you, contact the team with the page link and a description of the image. You do not need to post a child's name or personal details publicly to make a request.

Retention and your requests

Personal information should be kept only for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected or for applicable recordkeeping obligations. You may ask the team what information it holds about you, request access or correction, ask for deletion where applicable, object to processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. We may need proportionate information to verify that a request comes from the person concerned or their authorized representative.

You may also raise a complaint with Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office. A request to the foundation does not remove any rights available under applicable data protection law.

Policy updates

Changes will be published on this page with an updated date. See our Terms and Conditions for website use and donation information, or contact the foundation with a question.

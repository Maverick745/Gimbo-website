Our programs

Food, education, clothing, and care.

Five core programs address the everyday and long-term needs of the children and families we serve.

Community visits in Uganda.

Our approach

Support that meets the whole child

Support planned
with families.

Meals, school materials, clothing, hygiene support, and mentorship often need to be considered together. Our programs are organized around each child and family.

Every program depends on safe relationships, community visits, volunteers, and local leadership.

Food security

Food & nutrition

Regular, balanced meals help ensure hunger does not stand in the way of a school day or a normal childhood.

Food support is planned around community visits and family priorities, creating space for children to learn, play, and build strength.

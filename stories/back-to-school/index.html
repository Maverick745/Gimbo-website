Preparing Children for the School Term

School supplies, uniforms, meals, hygiene items, and transport can reduce practical barriers to attendance.

Gimbo Children’s Foundation

Practical information for donors and volunteers.

Support the work →

Preparing for a school term involves more than one item. Families may need help with books, pens, uniforms, shoes, hygiene supplies, meals, or transport, depending on their circumstances.

Gimbo Children's Foundation asks families and local leaders which needs are most urgent, then shares current priorities with people who want to help.

Support based on a confirmed need

The most useful donation is one the team can plan for. Before buying or sending supplies, supporters should ask which items are needed, how many are useful, and when they can be delivered.

This approach keeps the response practical and helps the team coordinate fairly with the families taking part.
